Blink the infrared sky.
Find what moves.

NASA's SPHEREx telescope photographs the whole sky every six months in 102 colours of infrared light. Skytrace flips between those pictures so anyone can spot nearby stars, brown dwarfs and other objects that move or change, then measure their light.
